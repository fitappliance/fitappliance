Advertiser partnerships

Last reviewed: 8 July 2026

This page is provided for advertiser review and affiliate-network compliance checks. FitAppliance is an Australian appliance fit-checking site that helps shoppers compare cavity, doorway and clearance requirements before visiting retailer product pages.

Traffic sources

FitAppliance is built for high-intent product research. Current and planned promotion is focused on organic search, direct visits, product-fit tools, sizing guides, editorial pages and compliant newsletter updates to users who subscribe on the site.

Organic searchAppliance fit, cavity sizing, brand clearance and doorway-access queries.

Compliance boundaries

  • No unsolicited email. FitAppliance does not send cold email campaigns or purchased-list promotions for advertiser offers.
  • No brand bidding. FitAppliance does not bid on advertiser trademarks, misspellings or domain variants unless an advertiser explicitly permits it in writing.
  • No cookie stuffing, forced clicks, toolbar traffic, cashback, coupon-code poaching or incentive traffic.
  • No misleading stock, price or guarantee claims. Retailer links and prices are shown only where product-page links or captured data are available.

Editorial and ranking policy

FitAppliance ranking starts with fit evidence: appliance dimensions, category, practical clearance buffers, doorway access and data availability. Affiliate relationships do not override whether a product appears to fit a shopper's cavity or delivery path.

Commercial relationships are disclosed through the public affiliate disclosure. Data handling and correction standards are documented in the methodology and editorial standards.
