Westinghouse Fridge dimensions and clearance requirements

Westinghouse fridge ranges cover narrow cabinets and very wide family units, so the airflow gaps matter early. Use this page to lock the clearances in before you shortlist by dimensions. Current guide figures are 30mm at each side, 60mm at the rear and 30mm above. The side gap is 10mm tighter than the usual category baseline. The rear allowance is 35mm deeper than the default figure. The top allowance is 20mm tighter than the default figure.

Installation Tips

  • Check the wall, benchtop and overhead cabinet as separate measurements before delivery day.
  • Leave enough room to level the cabinet after it is in position, not just enough room to slide it in.
  • Keep the installation manual beside the tape measure so the airflow note is checked against the real cavity.
